# Scale people matching by distinct facts

The people candidate reader separates complete distinct matching facts from
bounded raw evidence examples. The public processor and applier now pass the
fixture of 6,788 recording owners with two contributors, including interrupted
publication recovery, stale-owner rejection and genuine Archive-only withdrawal. People reads
explicitly select a larger complete-membership budget. Many-claim checkpoint
capacity remains required work.

The [pipeline receipt](evidence/people-contribution-pipeline-capacity-v1.json)
pins the executed source, assemblies and 129 passing cases. The earlier
[distinct-owner receipt](evidence/people-distinct-owner-capacity-v1.json) retains
its metadata-only capacity observation and reader measurements unchanged. These
checks establish publication and freshness mechanics, not identity accuracy or
throughput.

## Why repeated support must not exhaust matching facts

The [read-only catalog probe](evidence/people-candidate-capacity-v1.json) finds
one exact credit label in 5,671 taper/transferrer entries across 2,995 recordings
and 489 artists; another appears 5,483 times. Credit fields contain a literal
Charlie Miller reference on 6,788 recordings across 86 artists. Those labels can
contain compound credits, projects or namesakes. They justify a capacity test,
not a canonical identity decision or a claim of production failures.

The previous reader capped raw evidence at 5,000 contexts and 4 MiB. The current
people v4 contract instead returns complete distinct names, aliases, contact and
profile tuples, context presence, attachment uncertainty and kind conflicts.
Canonical kind, qualified identifiers and exact artist membership remain
separate. Other domains retain candidate facts v3; old policy attestations cannot
silently authorize the people v4 contract.

Repeated accounts do not consume the distinct-fact budget. Genuine semantic or
aggregate overflow remains explicit and blocks automatic acceptance. Bounded
original witnesses accompany complete matching facts, with total contexts,
witness counts and truncation reported separately.

## Preserve every owner's freshness

The reader validates original payload hashes and reads active accounts
sequentially. It retains every active owner observation, accepted credit/support
join, redirect and correction/retraction relationship. A bounded witness list
cannot omit freshness checks; the global people epoch alone cannot detect every
owner-only editorial change.

The earlier distinct-owner run used two service-published sentinel accounts and
6,786 mechanical relational copies. The copies test joins and capacity; they are
neither independently verified people nor upstream publications. That run
retained one raw witness, a 498-byte distinct matching aggregate and 1,072,663
bytes of owner observations. Its full snapshot serialized to 1,081,730 UTF-8
bytes; its acknowledgment ticket was 1,073,278 bytes. These are serialized sizes,
not network traffic, peak memory, database I/O or throughput measurements.

An unwitnessed owner's review change invalidates admission using the original
observations even while the candidate universe stays unchanged. Retraction of a
genuine service-published sentinel removes its unique semantics. The owner-clock
index builds keyed lookups in linear work and preserves the same observations;
it changes neither SQL nor lock order. Three new public cases, 21 existing owner
observation cases and the distinct-owner people fixture pass. No measured
speedup is claimed.

## Read complete contributor membership

The earlier metadata-only fixture stores 13,584 bindings, including 13,578 owner
bindings for a distinct union of 6,789 owners. Work keeps its default limit of
100 contributors and 10,000 stored bindings, so that read still correctly refuses
the fixture. All four people membership reads now explicitly select Work's
existing supported envelope of 100 contributors and 100,000 stored bindings.
Each contributor retains its own complete membership and original stamps.
Exceeding either bound still fails without a partial result.

The public pipeline also consumes extraction, proposal, policy and probe-owner
bindings. Its membership count must not be equated with the earlier 13,584-row
metadata fixture or the distinct owner union. The large test verifies that both
real probe contributors retain all 6,789 original seed owner keys, then makes new
processor and application calls after both memberships exist.

Contributor ownership also needs an exact admission identity. Archive can stop
consuming candidate keys when its wording becomes unresolved while local still
owns those keys. The union generation stays unchanged. Both people checkpoint
lookups now match the complete admitted contributor revisions, including retained
peers and empty tombstones, before applying the existing strict binding checks.
They preserve original preparations and tickets; neither UUID order nor attempt
creation order identifies the current admission.

Three focused ownership cases, the large pipeline case and 125 existing
regressions pass: 129 cases with no failures or skips. The solution builds with
zero errors and 25 existing warnings; the repository formatter check passes. The
public tests verify an accepted contact change, publication rollback and exact
retry, replay, stale original-owner rejection outside every raw witness,
inactive-policy acknowledgment, preservation of unresolved Archive support and
later genuine withdrawal of only that contributor. Empty peer revisions remain
unchanged across new processing passes.

## Resolve many-claim checkpoint capacity

The contribution budget does not change the 32 MiB preparation, batch, plan and
stage checkpoint limits or their repeated candidate snapshots. Many-claim
capacity and catalog-wide database cost remain required work before backfill.

The initial pipeline run, which later failed its final withdrawal assertion,
recorded a 12,329,300-byte preparation, 13,458,513-byte batch and 15,138,915-byte
stage as PostgreSQL JSON text. Those measurements remain attached to that failed
run. The passing V2 runs use minimal console logging and retain no fresh numeric
size output. Their success does not establish many-claim capacity, physical
storage cost, peak memory or throughput. Seed timings include assertions.

The earlier [aggregation receipt](evidence/people-candidate-aggregation-v1.json)
retains 126 matching/policy and 152 database checks, including 5,002 supporting
accounts beyond the old raw limits, unwitnessed aliases and contacts, semantic
overflow and nonpeople compatibility. The distinct-owner test covers the
additional owner-stamp burden. The
[people actor contract](../../../plans/active/2026-10-17-full-normalization/people-actor-evidence.md)
defines the reader and policy-version boundary. No identity matching policy is
activated.
